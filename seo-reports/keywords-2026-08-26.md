## 3. The finding that matters most

Delhi **video and film production** demand is an order of magnitude above every

| film production companies in delhi | 100 – 1K |
| explainer video company in delhi | 100 – 1K |
| **video production delhi** (current target) | **10 – 100** |

The two 1K–10K terms both carry -90% three-month and YoY changes, which looks
like a Google data artefact rather than a real collapse. Don't build on those
two rows. The 100–1K rows are clean.

**The gap is one word.** The page targets "video production delhi" and the
demand is at "video production **company** delhi" and "video production
**house** in delhi" — 10× the volume, both Low competition.

## 4. Recommendation

One change, on `video-production-delhi`: work "video production company" and
"production house" into the title, H1 and body copy so the page targets the
phrasing people actually type. Same service, same claims, same design — only
the wording of the head terms.

**Approved and applied 26 Aug.** What changed in `src/lib/landingPages.ts`,
on `video-production-delhi` only:

| Field | Before | After |

**The answer: there is no better phrase.** Nothing in this vocabulary clears 100
searches a month anywhere in India, and every city-level variant outside Delhi is
below the reporting threshold. The four architectural pages are not losing to a
wording choice — the category has almost no search demand in India at this scale.

Two consequences worth stating plainly:

1. **Do not rewrite the four architectural pages for keywords.** There is nothing to
   rank for. Any rewrite should be justified as better copy for the humans who arrive
   from elsewhere, not as SEO.
2. **This category will not come from search.** Architectural and interiors commissions
   in India appear to travel through architects, designers and publications rather than
   Google. Effort spent on those relationships is worth more than effort spent on these
   four pages.

The one mild signal: **Delhi** is the only city where interior/architecture terms
register at all (three separate terms at 10 – 100, two showing +900% YoY off a tiny
base). If any architectural page deserves attention, it is the Delhi one — and even
there the ceiling is a handful of searches a week.

The `-100%` on "interior photography delhi" and the `+900%` figures are both artefacts
of moving between tiny absolute numbers. Read them as noise, not trend.
